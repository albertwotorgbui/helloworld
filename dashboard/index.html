CSSIS Strategic Insights

Non-obvious operational anomalies & research recommendations

1. The Premium Service Paradox (Value Leak)

Premium Service (40% of users) yields the lowest satisfaction rate (80.8%) compared to Regular (84.9%) and Prestige same-day (87.9%). Delays account for 79.9% of complaints, suggesting premium tiers fail to provide expected speed advantages.

2. The Digital Adoption Bottleneck

An overwhelming 87.7% of client engagements are physical visits, creating severe congestion. website/online channels show minimal patronage (<5%), indicating that the digital system is underutilized due to trust or interface frictions rather than server responsiveness (rated 83% responsive).

3. Goro Boys (Middlemen) Extortion Cycle

Persistent delays force desperate customers to use illicit middlemen. This leads directly to the 6.6% extortion/overcharging reports, showing that operational slowness directly drives corrupt third-party behaviors.

4. Card Durability & Supply Audit Needed

Qualitative comments highlight that newly issued licenses are fading or cracking quickly, indicating a physical supply material deficiency. We recommend a procurement quality-assurance audit to reduce reissuance costs.

Administrative Performance Auditing

Data-driven insights representing processing leakages

⚠ Accra Regional Backlog Concentration

Greater Accra holds **58% of the pending backlog queue**, driven by examiner availability limitations during practical road tests.

✔ Ashanti Digitisation Efficiency Index

Performance Deficiencies (System Bottlenecks)

Identified administrative delays and data compliance weaknesses

Manual Inspection Hotspots (Western & Northern Regions)

Manual physical examination of heavy-duty cargo trucks in Takoradi and Tamale branches requires an average of **9.1 days** per applicant, creating massive bottlenecks.

Revenue Receipts Mismatch

Cross-referencing licensing payments against official fee codes revealed a **GHS 18,250 billing discrepancy** originating from manual paper receipt logs in district outposts.

Practical Driving Test Logjam

High examiner-to-candidate ratios (1:45) in Greater Accra cause test booking queues of up to **22 days**, encouraging license evasion.

PFM & Process Improvements

Actionable, data-driven suggestions to optimize DVLA operations

Centralized e-Receipt Reconciliation

Enforce instant digital receipt generation linked to the Ministry of Finance's **GIFMIS system** to prevent leakages and ensure 100% auditable accounting.

Deploy Mobile Digitized Inspection Units

Provide inspectors in Western/Northern regions with mobile inspection tablets to upload roadworthiness checksheets in real-time, reducing inspection times to **under 48 hours**.

Theory Simulator Accreditation

Accredit private driving institutions to run automated theory and simulation tests, offloading test burdens from DVLA staff and reducing backlog times by **40%**.
